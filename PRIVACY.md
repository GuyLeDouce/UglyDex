## 2. Information processed
Depending on features used, we may process public wallet addresses, token and contract identifiers, blockchain transaction hashes, timestamps, ownership history, NFT metadata and traits; wallet authentication signatures and sessions; optional profiles, usernames, linked Discord identifiers and submissions; game entries, results, achievements, contribution and $CHARM balance records; IP addresses, browser and device information, security and error logs, necessary cookies/local storage; and support correspondence. **No Ugly Labs monetary payments:** Ugly Labs does not process monetary transfers or NFT payments, or collect card information for NFT purchases. NFT transactions occur independently through OpenSea under its terms and applicable blockchain contracts. $CHARM is a fictional, non-liquid, off-chain internal game credit, not money or cryptocurrency.
